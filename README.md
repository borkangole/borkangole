## 🏗️ Featured Projects

### 🗺️ Spatial Decision Support System *(Thesis — In Progress, 72%)*
> A web-based GIS & analytics platform helping Philippine LGUs with evidence-based planning and social protection targeting. Leverages CBMS data, spatial analytics, and family network analysis to identify vulnerabilities at the family level across 6,370+ households in 24 barangays.

`React.js` `Flask` `Python` `PostgreSQL` `PostGIS` `Leaflet.js` `NetworkX` `Node.js`

---

### 🏘️ BrokeR_PH *(In Development, 45%)*
> A full-stack Real Estate Operating System (RE-OS) for the Philippine market — covering the entire brokerage lifecycle from lead acquisition, property listings, and scheduling to commission automation, document management, and real-time messaging.

`React.js` `Node.js` `Express.js` `PostgreSQL` `Socket.io` `Tailwind CSS` `JWT Auth` `Cloudinary`
